Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: attached Yeti sprite atlas. Refine ONLY the face readability of the same approved character in all 16 frames. Preserve the exact 4 by 4 layout, all full body silhouettes, hunched gorilla proportions, white fur colors, poses, facing direction, character size, ground baselines and transparent background. No new character design. Carefully clarify strong dark brow ridges, distinct amber eye with dark socket, broad gray gorilla nose with two dark nostrils, separated muzzle and jaw, contrasting dark mouth, ivory teeth/tusks, beard boundary. Make these forms legible when each character is reduced to about 110 pixels tall: intentional clean pixel clusters and stronger dark/light boundaries on the face, avoid noisy fine fur over the face. Keep the same fierce expressions and mouth open/closed for each pose. Retain true hand-pixel game art, crisp square pixels, no 3D rendering, no smooth vector, no blur. Clean away red/yellow/cyan extraction fringes around body silhouettes. Every entire sprite remains visible in its own uniform cell; no stray bits in neighboring cells. Actual transparent alpha background. Deliver edited atlas only, no labels or extra scene.

## Prompt original

Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset: TRUE pixel-art sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling survival game. Create a fearsome shaggy white YETI boss, distinctly hand-pixeled 16-bit game art, NOT a vector cartoon and NOT 3D. Original interpretation of an abominable snowman/gorilla: forward hunched powerful shoulders, head low between shoulders, long massive arms, chunky broad fists, short muscular bent legs, broad bare dark slate toes, shaggy matted off-white fur in irregular pixel clusters, blue-gray shadows, gray ape muzzle, amber hostile eyes, uneven mane and beard, subtle asymmetry. Silhouette organic and weighty, no sculpted round spheres or tube-like limbs, no horns, no blue crystal armor. Facing RIGHT in SIDE / THREE-QUARTER SIDE PROFILE suited for a side-scroller, NOT front-facing. Crisp square pixel clusters, compact game detail, limited coherent 12-16 color palette, dark broken contour, no smooth blur or antialias, light from upper left. Make a clean 4-column by 4-row UNIFORM sprite sheet with 16 separate frames of this EXACT SAME character. All cells identical size, equal margins, same camera, consistent character scale, consistent feet baseline. No overlaps between cells, no grid lines, no text, no shadow, transparent background. Entire bodies visible in every cell, ample space for raised arms. ROW 1: four successive walking frames, heavy lumbering stride facing right. ROW 2: four continuation walking frames, completing 8-frame walk cycle. ROW 3: cell1 idle hunched standing, cell2 idle breathing slightly expanded, cell3 roaring with raised open clawed hands and open snarling mouth, cell4 windup for overhead double-fist ground slam. ROW 4: cell1 impact crouch with BOTH huge fists planted on ground in front, cell2 holding a ragged chunk of pale blue ice overhead about to throw right, cell3 leaning forward blowing frosty breath with mouth open (NO external breath effect, body only), cell4 collapsed defeated on knees with hands low on ground. Prefer a square 1024x1024 atlas, each of 16 sprites occupies a logical 96x96 pixel cell visually enlarged with nearest neighbor. Deliver just the sprite atlas with actual alpha transparency, no scenery.
